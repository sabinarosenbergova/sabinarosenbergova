What does a town remember, and who gets to decide? Together with Tina Rosado and Dario Rodighiero, I work with the archive of *Svalbardposten*, the local newspaper of Longyearbyen: more than 16,700 articles published between 2006 and 2024, mapped as an interactive topography of the town's public conversations. Rather than presenting the map as a finished history, we bring it back to the community as a shared object of inquiry. I led the participatory workshop, where residents annotated the map, questioned its groupings and added what it could not see, and designed the project's ethics framework.
